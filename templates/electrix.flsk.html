Graph time frame

The hourly charge is the number of battery percentage gained during one hour of charging.

Graph navigation

Use the buttons below the graph to select/highlight a pricespot before or after the current one.

You can also use the keyboard to navigate within the chart.

Move the selection 15 mn backward Move the selection 15 mn forward
Ctrl Move the selection 1 hour backward Ctrl Move the selection 1 hour forward
Shift Move the selection 6 hours backward Shift Move the selection 6 hours forward

Information

The end charge must be greater than the current one; if not, the charging values will be modified automatically to ensure there is at least 1% difference between the initial and the final charge.
This correction is indicated by an orange pulse effect.

Privacy

All settings are saved locally on your browser private data storage. Absolutely no data is shared nor saved anywhere else.
